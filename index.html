About Pawa Mobile

Pawa Mobile Car Wash was founded on a simple idea: provide a top-quality, convenient car cleaning service that fits into our customers' busy lives. We use eco-friendly products and the latest techniques to ensure your vehicle gets the best treatment possible, all at your home or workplace. Our passion is making your car look its absolute best.

Why Choose Pawa Mobile?

Ultimate Convenience

We come to your home or office, saving you time and hassle. Book a time that works for you, and we'll be there.

Eco-Friendly Products

We use high-quality, biodegradable, and water-saving cleaning products that are safe for your car and the environment.

Professional Quality

Our experienced detailers are passionate about cars and committed to delivering a showroom-quality shine, every time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to provide water or power?

No, our mobile units are fully equipped with their own water and electricity supply. We can wash your car anywhere!

How long does a service take?

It depends on the package. An Express Wash takes about 30-45 minutes, while a Premium Shine can take up to 2 hours.

Do I need to be present?

You don't need to be there the whole time. Just unlock the car for interior access, and we'll handle the rest.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and mobile payments upon completion of the service.
